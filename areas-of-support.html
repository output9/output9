02 / AREAS OF SUPPORT

A broader view
of human
wellbeing.

Our philanthropic interests span six areas of human need and possibility. Each is informed by the same underlying belief: people and communities should have the means to build better futures for themselves.

01 / 06 A FAMILY’S FUTURE

Child & Family Welfare

Supporting the wellbeing and stability of children and families.

A strong foundation for life begins with the conditions in which children and families grow. This area of interest encompasses organizations whose work attends to family wellbeing, access to essential support, and the ability to build stability over time.

About grant eligibility ↗
02 / 06 ACCESS TO LEARNING

Education

Helping open doors through learning and opportunity.

Education creates possibility that extends beyond a single moment. We consider work in this area through the lens of access, preparation and the role learning can play in helping people shape more independent futures.

About grant eligibility ↗
03 / 06 PATHS TOWARD INDEPENDENCE

Poverty Focused Development

Advancing lasting solutions to economic hardship, at home and abroad.

Our mission places particular emphasis on organizations helping people move beyond poverty. We recognize the importance of community led approaches, both domestically and internationally, that expand the means for people to improve their own circumstances.

About grant eligibility ↗
04 / 06 HEALTH & WELLBEING

Physical & Mental Health

Recognizing that wellbeing is essential to possibility.

Physical and mental health are fundamental to a person’s capacity to participate, learn, work and thrive. We have an interest in organizations responding thoughtfully to needs in both dimensions of health.

About grant eligibility ↗
05 / 06 COMMUNITY & MEANING

Religious & Spiritual Development

Acknowledging the role of spiritual life and community.

Our stated areas of philanthropic support also include religious and spiritual development, reflecting a broad view of human and community welfare. As with all areas of interest, support is considered within the foundation’s published nonprofit eligibility framework.

About grant eligibility ↗
06 / 06 RESPONSE & RECOVERY

Disaster Relief

Making room for help when circumstances change suddenly.

In times of disaster, the ability of communities to recover can depend on timely, organized support. Disaster relief is one of the foundation’s established areas of interest, alongside its longer term focus on human welfare and opportunity.
