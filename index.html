01 /THE FOUNDATION

Progress begins
with possibility.

We believe in the extraordinary capacity of individuals and communities to build a better future for themselves.

Founded and endowed by Richard and Judith Andrews in 1986, the Andrews Foundation is a family foundation devoted to supporting organizations that create opportunity, strengthen communities, and help people move toward lasting independence.

03 /GRANTMAKING

A thoughtful route
to support.

We welcome initial inquiries from eligible nonprofit organizations whose work aligns with our areas of interest. The process begins with a simple introduction.

01THE FIRST STEP

Begin with an inquiry.

Tell us about your organization, the people it serves, and the work you believe needs support.

02OUR REVIEW

Then, a conversation may follow.

We review initial inquiries and may invite selected organizations to complete a full application. Please allow up to six weeks for a response.

Funding is available to eligible 501(c)(3) organizations only.
