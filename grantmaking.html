01 /ELIGIBILITY AT A GLANCE

The right work.
The right fit.

WHO MAY APPLY

Registered charitable organizations.

The Andrews Foundation provides grants to organizations recognized as 501(c)(3) nonprofits. It does not award grants to individuals.

WHAT WE CONSIDER

Work aligned with our interests.

Our focus areas include child and family welfare, education, domestic and international poverty focused development, physical and mental health, religious and spiritual development, and disaster relief.

02 /FUNDING LIMITATIONS

Understanding what falls outside our scope.

The foundation generally does not provide grants for the following. Reviewing these limitations can help determine whether an initial inquiry is appropriate.

02

04 /COMMON QUESTIONS

The things you
may want to know.

Can individuals apply for funding?+

No. The foundation makes grants to eligible 501(c)(3) organizations, not individuals.

Do we need to submit a full application first?+

No. Begin with the initial grant inquiry form. The foundation may invite selected organizations to submit a complete application after reviewing the inquiry.

How long does the initial review take?+

Please allow up to six weeks for a response to your initial inquiry.

Can we send our grant request through the contact page?+

No. The contact form does not accept grant requests. Use the dedicated initial inquiry process instead.

Does the foundation support work outside Houston?+

Its published areas of interest include both domestic and international poverty focused development. A specific limitation applies to food banks and assistance ministries providing emergency assistance outside the Houston area. Please review all stated priorities and limitations before making an inquiry.
