01 /THE BEGINNING
1986THE YEAR OUR STORY BEGAN

An enduring commitment, rooted in family.

The Andrews Foundation was founded and endowed by Richard and Judith Andrews in Houston, Texas, in 1986. Its purpose was, and remains, to help people and communities help themselves.

That conviction gives the foundation its direction. Rather than viewing need as a permanent condition, we look toward organizations working to create opportunity and equip people to make lasting changes in their own lives.

OUR MISSION

We believe people
should have the means
to shape their own future.

Our philanthropic attention is especially directed toward organizations that help people living with limited financial resources gain greater independence and work toward a permanent escape from poverty.
